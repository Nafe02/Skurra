SKURRA

Scan. Clear. Stay in control.

Skurra finds what you can clear on a Mac or Windows. Caches go with the brush. Everything else waits until you decide.

  • Your files stay on this computer.
  • Nothing is thrown away until you tick it and say yes.
  • Thrown-away things go to the Trash or Recycle Bin. You can put them back.
  • Downloads, Desktop, and Documents stay closed until you allow them.

File names are not sent anywhere. Read the code.

TOOLS

What Skurra actually does

Each one stays on its own screen until the work is finished.

SCAN

A green ring, with the percent

Scan measures caches, leftover files, and apps. The least used apps sit at the top. A leftover is not the app.

BRUSH

Caches go with the brush

Caches are swept in place. You still see the brush, the amount, and what went.

DISK

This folder, on the whole disk

Search looks through the whole computer. The ring shows the open folder against the whole disk. Switch between a list and a grid.

LOCATION

A small window, then the folder

The window names the file and the path. Show in Finder on a Mac, Show in File Explorer on Windows.

BIN

Trash and Recycle Bin

See what is in the bin, delete the ones you tick, or empty it. Papers leave the bin. You can still put things back until you do.

UPDATE

Update now, on both

A new version installs from inside Skurra and restarts. The same button is on Mac and Windows.

DISK SPACE

This folder, on the whole disk.

The outer ring is the disk. The inner ring is the folder you opened. Folders keep the colour. Files stay one grey.

  • Search looks through the whole computer, not only the open folder.
  • What you have not used in the longest time sits at the top.
  • Photos and screenshots show a thumbnail. The list can also be a grid.

FIRST OPEN

Your computer asks once.

Skurra is not registered with Apple or Microsoft yet. After that first yes, later versions use Update now.

MAC

Download for Mac
  1. Open Skurra.dmg and drag Skurra into Applications.
  2. If macOS says it was blocked, open Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

WINDOWS

Download for Windows
  1. Double-click Skurra.exe.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
