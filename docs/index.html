Look first. Then clean.

Most cleaners delete first and apologise later. Skurra does it the other way round.

It scans and explains

Caches, leftover app data and your installed apps, each with its size, when it was last used, and a plain-English reason it is on the list.

Safe, less important, important

App data is sorted into three groups. Anything that holds real work is locked so you cannot tick it by mistake.

Trash first, delete later

Apps and app data go to the Trash or Recycle Bin, so you can change your mind. Caches are cleared on the spot. Everything is written in a diary.

Opening it the first time

Skurra is not registered with Apple or Microsoft yet, which costs money. So your computer double-checks with you once.

Mac Fastest: one line, no warnings

Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal), paste this, press Return. It downloads Skurra, puts it in Applications and opens it.

curl -fsSL https://nafe02.github.io/Skurra/install.sh | bash
or, with the download button
  1. Open Skurra.dmg and drag Skurra into Applications, then open it.
  2. When it says “Skurra” Not Opened, click Done.
  3. System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Skurra was blocked, click Open Anyway.
Windows
  1. Double-click Skurra.exe.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears: More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks.
