Skurra

A free storage cleaner that shows you exactly what it would remove, and why, before it touches anything.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Free. No account. Works on Macs with Intel or Apple chips, and on Windows 10 and 11.

Opening it the first time

Mac

Open the downloaded Skurra.dmg and drag Skurra into Applications. Then right-click Skurra and choose Open. If your Mac says it can’t verify the developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Windows

Double-click Skurra.exe. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks. You only do this once.

Those warnings appear because Skurra isn’t registered with Apple or Microsoft yet, which costs money. The app is the same either way.

What it does

  • Scans caches, leftover app data and your installed apps, and sorts them into safe, less important and important.
  • Caches are cleared on the spot. Apps and app data go to the Trash or Recycle Bin first, so you can change your mind.
  • Keeps a diary of everything it has moved, so you can always see what happened.
  • Never touches your contacts, mail, messages, photos or backups.
